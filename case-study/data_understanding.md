# Data Understanding

## Business Overview

The Sample Superstore dataset represents sales transactions for a retail company.

Each row represents one line item purchased by a customer.

---

## Business Goal

The objective of this project is to identify opportunities to increase profitability while maintaining sales growth.

---

## Dataset Overview

The dataset contains information about:

- Orders
- Customers
- Products
- Sales
- Profit
- Discount
- Geography
- Shipping

---

## Potential Business Questions

1. Which region generates the most sales?

2. Which region is the most profitable?

3. Which products lose money?

4. Does discounting reduce profit?

5. Which customer segments are most valuable?

6. Which categories should the company invest in?
